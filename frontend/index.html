Features

Why WrapSynth?

Monero is the best money in crypto and the most isolated. No lending, no liquidity, no composability — just an asset you have to sell to ever use. WrapSynth ends that, without asking you to trust a bridge operator with your coins.

Put your XMR to work

Monero has no native DeFi capabilities. wsXMR does. Earn yield, post collateral, or provide liquidity while keeping full exposure to the asset you actually want to hold.

Outlast the delistings

As exchanges drop Monero jurisdiction by jurisdiction, holders lose their on-ramps. WrapSynth is a path into EVM liquidity that doesn't depend on any CEX choosing to list you.

Private in, composable out

Hold a private store of value, and touch public rails only when you choose to. Your net worth isn't broadcast by default.

Peer-to-peer atomic exchanges

Mints and burns settle directly between you and an LP — the secret commitment is verified on-chain. Monero is held in a 2-of-2 multisig until the exchange completes, so no single party ever has unilateral custody.

Always redeemable

Every wsXMR is backed by 150% collateral in LP vaults, with a 120% liquidation threshold and a 10% liquidator bonus keeping the system solvent against real-time price feeds. Your claim is always good.

Live on mainnet

Real XMR has minted and burned wsXMR on Gnosis Chain mainnet — a full cycle, live. Mints only clear when the on-chain cryptographic check passes, and the collateral ratio is a value you read straight from the contract.

Process

How it works

Four steps to ferry your Monero to DeFi — trustless on both sides of the swap.

Mint wsXMR

1

Initiate swap

You create a mint request with a secret commitment and send XMR to the LP's Monero address.

2

LP confirms receipt

The LP verifies the XMR off-chain and marks the mint request READY on-chain.

3

Reveal secret

You reveal your secret to claim wsXMR — verified on-chain with Ed25519 operations (Monero-compatible).

4

Receive wsXMR

The contract mints wsXMR to your wallet, backed by real XMR and guaranteed by the LP's collateral in their vault.

Burn wsXMR

1

Initiate burn

You create a burn request with a secret commitment and send wsXMR to the contract.

2

LP locks XMR

The LP locks your XMR on Monero with a secret hash and proposes the lock on-chain.

3

Confirm receipt

You verify the Monero transaction and confirm the lock on-chain, binding both parties.

4

Receive XMR

The LP reveals the secret to finalize the burn. Your XMR unlocks and their collateral is released.
